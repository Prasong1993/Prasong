# IRIS
For each step: CHECK → DO → VERIFY. If not met, record error, checkpoint, correct/retry and verify again. Do not advance until PASS.
## Five steps
1. แนวคิด
2. ออกแบบ
3. ทำงานจริง
4. ส่งผล
5. ตรวจสอบบนพื้นที่ ChatGPT
## Delivery
Deliver only after all five steps pass and final expected-vs-actual verification is VERIFIED.
## Evidence
Structure, implementation, execution and validation are distinct. Source evidence is not runtime proof. Failures are retained.
